# SYSTEM DIRECTIVE: Myntra VoC Growth & Discovery Intelligence Engine (15k+ Scale)

## 1. STRATEGIC CONTEXT & NON-NEGOTIABLE CONSTRAINTS
- **Target Platform:** Myntra (Fashion E-commerce, India).
- **Core Business Metric:** Increase the 30-Day Wishlist-to-Purchase Conversion Rate (% of users who buy $\ge 1$ item from their wishlist within 30 days of saving).
- **CRITICAL CONSTRAINT:** NO MONETARY INCENTIVES. The solution space strictly forbids discounts, price-drop alerts, coupons, cashback, or wallet points. 
- **Analytical Objective:** Ingest, clean, structure, and quantify 15,000+ unstructured user discussions across public channels to isolate the root non-monetary psychological, informational, and visual barriers causing users to stall or abandon saved products.

## 2. HIGH-VOLUME INGESTION & FILTERING ARCHITECTURE (15,000+ RECORDS)

### A. Data Ingestion Sources
1. **App Store & Google Play Store Reviews (Target: ~10,000 records):**
   - Package: `com.myntra.android` / Apple App ID: `907394059`.
   - Priority Ratings: 1-star, 2-star, 3-star, and 4-star reviews.
   - Core Search Tokens: `wishlist`, `saved`, `bag`, `cart`, `hesitate`, `confused`, `fit`, `size`, `material`, `styling`, `pairing`, `color`, `expensive`, `quality`, `return`, `didn't buy`.
2. **Reddit Indian Fashion Communities (Target: ~3,500 records):**
   - Subreddits: `r/IndianFashionAddicts`, `r/TwoXIndia`, `r/DesiFragranceAddicts`, `r/IndianSkincareAddicts`, `r/delhi`, `r/bangalore`.
   - Search Queries: `"Myntra wishlist"`, `"Myntra sizing"`, `"Myntra return"`, `"buying from Myntra"`, `"how to style"`, `"worth buying"`, `"fabric quality Myntra"`.
3. **YouTube Fashion Content Comments (Target: ~1,500 records):**
   - Top 50 Myntra Haul, Try-on Review, and "Myntra Hits & Misses" videos.

### B. Preprocessing & Batch Partitioning
- **Deduplication:** Hash-based deduplication on raw text.
- **Language Normalization:** Transliterate and normalize Hinglish/slang (e.g., *"kapda kharab hai"*, *"fit sahi nahi baitha"*, *"too pricey for this quality"*).
- **Price Separation Filter:** Flag and isolate entries that are *purely* price-seeking (e.g., *"waiting for BBD 70% off"*). Track their volume for intent share, but route only non-monetary hesitations into the opportunity matrix.

## 3. MULTI-DIMENSIONAL CLASSIFICATION TAXONOMY

For every ingested review/comment, extract and classify across the following 5 dimensions:

### Dimension 1: Wishlist Behavioral Intent
1. `GENUINE_PURCHASE_INTENT`: High willingness to buy, blocked by specific residual uncertainties (fit, pairing, occasion).
2. `AESTHETIC_BOOKMARKING`: Using the wishlist as a Pinterest board / aspirational moodboard with zero near-term intent.
3. `SHORTLIST_COMPARISON`: Holding 3+ similar items (e.g., 4 white oversized shirts) to deliberate between them later.
4. `PRICE_SPECULATION`: Pure price-drop waiting (isolated per constraint).

### Dimension 2: Non-Monetary Root Uncertainty & Hesitation Vector
1. `FIT_AND_SILHOUETTE_AMBIGUITY`:
   - Inconsistent brand sizing (e.g., Roadster M vs. Mango M).
   - Inability to gauge garment drape on non-model bodies (height, curves, torso length).
   - Skepticism over studio lighting vs. real-life appearance.
2. `STYLING_AND_PAIRABILITY_ANXIETY`:
   - Lack of wardrobe compatibility ("What bottom/shoes do I wear with this?").
   - Uncertainty if the piece is versatile across multiple occasions or a one-time wear.
   - Isolation friction (item looks great alone on a mannequin, but hard to assemble into a real look).
3. `FABRIC_AND_TACTILE_DOUBT`:
   - Inability to verify material weight, sheer/transparency, breathability, and shrinkage.
   - Confusing composition labels (e.g., "polyester blend" with no tactile context).
4. `SOCIAL_VALIDATION_LAG`:
   - Need for external affirmation (friends, partner, peer community) before committing.
5. `OCCASION_DISCONNECT`:
   - Liking an item aesthetically, but lacking an immediate event/reason to justify wearing it.
6. `COMPARISON_PARALYSIS`:
   - Fatigue from opening multiple tabs to cross-examine minor spec differences across shortlisted brands.

### Dimension 3: Offline & Cross-Platform Workarounds
Extract external actions users take to resolve hesitation:
1. `WHATSAPP_PEER_VALIDATION`: Screenshotting and sharing to group chats for opinions.
2. `YOUTUBE_TRYON_SEARCH`: Searching third-party video creators to see real fabric movement and fit.
3. `CANVA_PINTEREST_COLLAGING`: Manually creating moodboards to see if an item matches their wardrobe.
4. `BRACKETING_AS_WORKAROUND`: Intentionally buying 2 sizes with the premeditated plan to return one.

### Dimension 4: User Cohort & Category Segmentation
- **User Segment:**
  - `STUDENT_GEN_Z` (High trend awareness, social-validation heavy, tight budget).
  - `WORKING_PROFESSIONAL` (Time-poor, versatility/capsule focused, high intent on fit consistency).
  - `TIER_2_ASPIRATIONAL` (High brand-authenticity and fabric-durability skepticism).
- **Product Category:**
  - `WESTERN_CASUAL`, `WESTERN_FORMAL_WORKWEAR`, `ETHNIC_OCCASION`, `FOOTWEAR`, `STREETWEAR`.

---

## 4. QUANTIFICATION & OPPORTUNITY SCORING ALGORITHM

Aggregate the 15,000+ records and calculate the prioritized opportunity score for each friction vector:

$$\text{Opportunity Score} = \text{Frequency Share (\%)} \times \text{Severity (1–5)} \times \text{Non-Monetary Solvability (1–5)}$$

- **Frequency Share (%):** $\frac{\text{Mentions of Friction } X}{\text{Total Analyzed Non-Monetary Mentions}} \times 100$
- **Friction Severity (1–5):** Degree to which this friction completely terminates the purchase decision ($1 = \text{minor delay}$, $5 = \text{permanent drop-off/abandonment}$).
- **Non-Monetary Solvability (1–5):** Feasibility of resolving this uncertainty purely through UI/UX, AI vision, social proof, or workflow interventions ($1 = \text{requires physical touch/price cut}$, $5 = \text{fully solvable digitally}$).
